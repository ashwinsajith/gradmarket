02 — Limits

What this can't see.

Sampling frame
Five applicant tracking systems: Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable and Workday. Most of the FTSE 100 hire through other systems, so most of the FTSE 100 isn't here.
Recall
Early-careers detection finds about 81% of graduate roles on a 50-posting held-out sample, so some real roles are missed. It errs towards leaving a role out rather than showing one that isn't early-careers.
Ages
Ages are counted from the day a role was first observed. Anything already live when its board was first observed is shown as a minimum, with a “+” — its real age is unknown, only that it's at least that old.
Content
Job descriptions belong to the employers and aren't republished here. Every link goes to the employer's own posting.
